Twenty-eight years on the same corner

Lagune started as four plastic tables and a grill on the pavement of Treichville. Marie-Jeanne, who still runs the kitchen, cooked whatever her brother brought back from the lagoon that morning. The menu has not really changed since.

We buy from the same three fishermen every day, we grill over wood rather than charcoal, and we close when the fish runs out. The terrace seats sixty, the music starts at eight, and nobody is ever rushed through a meal.

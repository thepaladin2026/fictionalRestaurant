Where we go

Three routes we run all year. Departures every two weeks.

Assinie, Côte d'Ivoire

3 days

A sandbar between the Atlantic and the lagoon. Pirogue crossings at dawn, grilled fish at noon, and nothing much to do in between.

Learn More

Man, Côte d'Ivoire

5 days

The green hills of the west: waterfalls, the liana bridges of the Dan country, and markets that start before sunrise.

Adrar, Mauritania

8 days

Dunes, date palm oases and the library towns of Chinguetti, where manuscripts have been kept by the same families for centuries.
